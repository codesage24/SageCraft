# SageCraft

This is the repository for SageCraft, an AddOn for Ascension (Bronzebeard) for Tracking your Profession recipes across all your characters. 

## Setup

1. Download SageCraft.zip from the releases section.
2. Unzip the file.
3. Move the `SageCraft` folder to your World of Warcraft AddOns directory. This is usually located at:
   - Windows: `C:\Program Files\Ascension Launcher\resources\client\Interface\AddOns\`

## How to Use

When logged on a character you can open any profession window and on the left of the title should be a scope icon.
Clicking this icon will import all known recipes for that character.

When you hover over a recipe in your bags or on a loot roll you will see which of your characters know that recipe.

## Commands

- `/sc` - Explains the various command options.
- `/sc who <recipe name>` - Displays which of your characters know the specified recipe.
- `/sc recipes` - Opens a window showing all your tracked profession recipes across all your characters.
- `/sc clear` - Clears all saved data for the character your on.`
